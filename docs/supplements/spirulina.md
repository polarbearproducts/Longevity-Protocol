# Spirulina

## Key Purpose

Adding **Spirulina** is a heavy-duty move for your structural health, particularly for **heavy metal detoxification** and **lipid management**. It is essentially a "multivitamin in algae form," packed with protein, B-vitamins, and phycocyanin, a potent anti-inflammatory.

## Dosage and Frequency

* **Mon/Thu/Sat:** Add 1 tsp (3 grams) Spirulina to your breakfast bowls
* **Why:** This gives you a pulse of heavy metal detox three times a week without making every meal taste like algae.

## Details

Because Spirulina has a very strong "oceanic" flavor and high mineral density, we have to integrate it carefully so it doesn't clash with your tea protocol or overload your kidneys.

### **1. The Clinical Benefits for Your Plan**

* **Vascular Protection:** Spirulina helps reduce LDL cholesterol and triglycerides, which synergizes with the "plaque-clearing" goals of your **MenaQ7**.
* **Heavy Metal Defense:** Since you are a regular tea drinker (and all teas can contain trace amounts of lead or aluminum from the soil), Spirulina acts as a **chelator**, binding to those metals in the gut and helping you excrete them.
* **The "Nitric Oxide" Boost:** Like the beet powder we discussed, Spirulina contains amino acids like **L-arginine** that support blood vessel dilation.

### **2. Integration Strategy**

Since the flavor is very "seaweed-forward," I recommend **against** putting it in your teas. Instead, use one of these two methods:

#### **Method A: Breakfast Bowl Integration (Recommended)**

Spirulina's flavor is easily masked by the **acidity of Greek yogurt and the sweetness of blueberries** in your High-Protein Probiotic Bowl.

* **The Mix:** Stir **1 teaspoon (approx. 3g)** of Spirulina powder directly into the yogurt.
* **The Synergy:** The **sacrificial calcium** in the yogurt will manage any trace oxalates in the Spirulina, and the fats in the yogurt will help you absorb the fat-soluble vitamins (A and K) in the algae.

#### **Method B: The Matcha-Spirulina Latte (Experimental)**

If you are doing the **Soy Milk Cold Brew Matcha**, you can add Spirulina here.

* **The Flavor:** The grassiness of the Matcha and the creaminess of the Soy Milk are the only things that can successfully hide Spirulina.
* **The Pro-Tip:** Use a **smaller dose** (1/2 tsp) first. If you use too much, the drink will taste like the ocean.

### **3. Oxalate & Mineral Contradiction**

Spirulina is relatively low in oxalates, but it is **exceptionally high in minerals**

* **The Conflict:** You are already doing a high-mineral tea rotation.
* **The Fix:** Do not take Spirulina at the exact same time as your **Olive Leaf** or **Hawthorn** hot preps. The tannins in those teas might bind to the iron in the Spirulina, making both less effective.
* **Timing:** Keep Spirulina in the **Mid-Day window** with your Matcha or Bowl B.

## **Sourcing: The 2026 Purity Standard**

Spirulina is an "accumulator," meaning it absorbs whatever is in the water it grows in.

* **The Risk:** Low-quality Spirulina can contain **BMAA** (a neurotoxin) or **heavy metals**.
* **The Gold Standard:** Look for **California-grown** (like Earthrise) or **Hawaiian-grown** (like Nutrex) brands. They use closed-loop systems and 3rd-party testing to ensure they are free of microcystins and lead.
